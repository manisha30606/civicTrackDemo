    How the full app works

    Reporting and collaboration

    Citizens submit a report with a map pin and optional photos. They can browse nearby issues, upvote, follow progress, comment on their own cases, leave resolution feedback, and reopen a case if needed.

    Assignments and deadlines

    Admins assign reports to workers in the relevant department. Workers update assigned cases. The dashboard shows pending, in-progress, resolved, overdue, and recurring cases.

    Duplicate and recurring reports

    Two reports about the same active issue can share one main case and status. When a repaired issue returns, a new case can link to the closed report and keep its own deadline and assignment.

    Architecture and access

    The full app uses Next.js, TypeScript, Express, MongoDB Atlas, and Socket.IO. It enforces role and case permissions on the API. A separate Super Admin portal manages accounts and application settings.
